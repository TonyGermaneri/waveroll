Waveroll

A rolling sampler. It keeps the last sixteen bars of whatever it hears and lets you select a bar-quantised region of it. It makes no sound of its own.

Needs a microphone or interface, and WebGPU. Run starts the internal transport; capture follows the transport, so nothing is recorded until something says it is playing. Enable MIDI and a DAW's clock takes over.
